## 2026-11-15 — Welcome to HyperGrid Explorer
HyperGrid Explorer is a modern viewer for OpenSimulator and the Hypergrid, written in Rust with a Vulkan renderer.

This is an early test build: thank you for trying it. News, downloads and support are on hypergridexplorer.com.
[Visit the website](https://hypergridexplorer.com)
